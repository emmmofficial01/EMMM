MEET EMMM

Emmm is an award-winning Nigerian-born, Canadian-based Afro-Fusion artist, songwriter and live entertainer. Since beginning his professional music career in 2020, he has developed a sound that pairs sensual melodies with infectious African rhythms and high-energy Afrobeats.

Influenced by Victony, Rema and Omah Lay, Emmm creates music that moves between intimacy and celebration, connecting with audiences across cultures. Based in Barrie, Ontario and signed to Diamondd Records, he has released projects including Gullible 2, 24, Serenade and WET.
