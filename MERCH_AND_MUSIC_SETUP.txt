EMMM WEBSITE: MERCH SIGNUP + MUSIC

1. Deploy this folder using Netlify connected to GitHub. Netlify Forms should detect the re3elz-merch-alerts form during deployment. Check Site > Forms for submissions and configure email notifications in Netlify. Form detection and availability depend on your Netlify plan/settings.
2. Subscribing stores an email submission in Netlify when deployed properly; it does NOT automatically send a launch campaign. Export contacts or connect an email marketing service and send launch messages only to consenting subscribers. Include unsubscribe controls in marketing emails.
3. Opening index.html locally will NOT save Netlify form submissions. Test on the deployed Netlify website.
4. Music uses an official Spotify artist embed on the homepage. Browsers block unprompted audible autoplay. Visitors must click play; Spotify determines whether any play qualifies as a stream. Direct MP3 background audio does not count as a Spotify or Apple Music stream.
5. EMMM’S CRIB contact is on the Crib page and the main Bookings page.
